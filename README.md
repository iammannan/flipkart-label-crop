## ⚡ Why Use Flipkart Label Crop & Cutter?

When you download shipping labels from **Flipkart Seller Hub** or **Shopsy**, each page contains a small 4×6 shipping label stacked on top of a full A4 tax invoice. 

Sending that raw A4 PDF straight to a 4×6 thermal printer squeezes the entire page into a tiny sticker, rendering barcodes and QR codes unreadable by **Ekart Logistics**, **Delhivery**, **Shadowfax**, or **Xpressbees** scanners.

**Flipkart Label Crop** is an intelligent, high-speed **quick label crop** tool that:
1. **Automatically cuts the tax invoice** away from the shipping label.
2. **Crops the shipping label tight to its borders**, eliminating unnecessary white space.
3. **Resizes and centers the label** for standard 4×6 inch (100×150 mm) thermal rolls or A4 paper.
4. **Preserves 100% vector quality**, ensuring barcodes and QR codes print razor-sharp at 203 DPI.
5. **Sorts orders by SKU and Ekart courier partner** to slash warehouse packing time in half.

## 📖 How to Crop Flipkart Shipping Labels in 3 Steps

1. **Download PDF from Flipkart Seller Hub**: In your Flipkart Seller panel, go to **Orders &rarr; Dispatch After Packing** and download your shipping label PDF.
2. **Drop File into Flipkart Label Crop Tool**: Visit [Flipkart Label Crop](https://labelcropai.web.app/flipkart-label-crop) and drag & drop your label PDF into the upload area.
3. **Select Format & Print**: Choose **4×6 in** for thermal rolls or **A4 Smart Fit / 4-up**, customize sorting by SKU or courier, and click **Download PDF** or **Print**.
